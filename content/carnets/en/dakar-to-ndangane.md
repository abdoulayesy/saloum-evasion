Ndangane is the gateway to the Saloum Delta, about 150 km from Dakar and 100 km from Blaise-Diagne International Airport (AIBD) [to check].

## Private transfer

The simplest option after a flight: a driver meets you at the airport or your Dakar hotel. Usual time from AIBD: about 2 hours [to check]; from Dakar: 3 hours or more depending on traffic [to check]. You can add it when booking your stay.

## Taxi

Airport and Dakar taxis will drive to Ndangane. Agree on the price before leaving [price to check, in CFA francs].

## Rental car

The usual route takes the toll motorway to Mbour, then the road via Joal and Fimela to Ndangane [route to check].

## Public transport

Buses and minibuses link Dakar to Mbour and the Saloum villages: the cheapest option, but the longest [to check].

## From Ndangane to the islands

Pirogues leave the Ndangane jetty for Mar Lodj and the other islands in a few minutes [to check: time, schedules and price].

## Our tips

- Leave Dakar early in the morning to avoid traffic.
- Carry cash in CFA francs for tolls and the crossing.
- Keep water in the car.
