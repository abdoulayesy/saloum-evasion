Mar Lodj is one of the most endearing islands of the delta. For places to stay on the island, see our [Mar Lodj](/en/destinations/mar-lodj) page; this guide answers practical questions before crossing.

## Getting there

The pirogue crossing leaves the Ndangane jetty and takes a few minutes [to check].

## Getting around without cars

There are no paved roads or cars on the island [to check]: walk, rent a bike or ride a horse or donkey cart.

## Things to do in Mar Lodj

- Walk through the villages and meet the residents.
- Walk to the great baobabs and rice fields.
- Swim on the quiet beaches along the bolongs.
- Take a pirogue to watch birds at sunset.

## How long to stay

One day is enough for a first visit; one or two nights let you enjoy the quiet evenings.

## Respect the island

Take your waste back to the mainland and ask before photographing residents.
