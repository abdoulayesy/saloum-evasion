The Saloum Delta is a maze of sea channels, mangroves and islands, listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site. People come for the light, the calm and the encounters. Here are the ten experiences we recommend most often; all our activities are on the [Sine-Saloum activities and excursions](/en/experiences) page.

## Take a pirogue through the bolongs

Bolongs are the saltwater channels winding between the mangroves. A pirogue trip from Ndangane glides slowly past herons, kingfishers and mangrove roots covered in oysters. Allow half a day [to check]; trips follow the tides.

## Watch the birds of Bird Island

Off the delta, Bird Island hosts large colonies of seabirds. The best period depends on the species [to check with the team: nesting months]. Bring binoculars and a hat.

## Spend a day on Mar Lodj, the car-free island

A short pirogue ride from Ndangane [to check], Mar Lodj is explored on foot or by cart: villages, baobabs, rice fields and quiet beaches.

## Taste mangrove oysters

Harvested from mangrove roots, the oysters are grilled over a wood fire. A meal with a local family is the best way to taste them.

## Visit Joal-Fadiouth and its shell island

Fadiouth is built on shells and linked to Joal by a long wooden bridge. Its mixed Christian and Muslim cemetery tells the story of the communities living together.

## Meet the villages and Serer culture

A village visit with a local guide helps you understand Serer traditions respectfully.

## Explore the bush by quad or on horseback

A quad or horse ride crosses the savannah and salt flats towards Palmarin.

## Watch the sunset over the delta

From the Ndangane jetty or a pirogue, sunsets over the bolongs are among the finest in Senegal.

## Go fishing with village fishermen

Joining a traditional fishing trip is possible on request [to check: conditions and season].

## Slow down

Leave room in your plans to read by the water, swim and listen to the birds.
