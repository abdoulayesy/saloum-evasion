Sine-Saloum can be visited all year round, but the experience changes a lot with the season.

## The two main seasons

**The dry season** usually runs from November to June [to check]: clear skies, few mosquitoes, cooler nights in December and January.

**The rainy season** runs roughly from July to October [to check]: short heavy showers, humid heat, very green landscapes, fewer visitors.

## Month by month

- **November to February**: pleasant weather, arrival of migratory birds [to check].
- **March to June**: rising heat, calm sea, good conditions for pirogue trips.
- **July to October**: rains, lush vegetation, very quiet atmosphere.

## Tides and pirogue trips

The delta lives with the tides. Your guide picks the departure time according to the day’s tide: keep some flexibility in your plans.

## Birds

Birds are present all year, but the big gatherings depend on the season [to check with the team: species and months].

## What to pack

Dry season: hat, sunscreen, a light sweater for December evenings. Rainy season: quick-drying clothes, mosquito repellent and suitable shoes.
