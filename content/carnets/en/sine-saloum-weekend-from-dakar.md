Sine-Saloum is the ideal weekend escape from Dakar: close enough to leave after work, far enough to change scenery completely.

## Friday: leave at the right time

Leave Dakar before 4 pm or after 8 pm to avoid peak traffic [to check]. Allow about 3 hours to Ndangane [to check].

## Saturday: pirogue and island

- **Morning**: pirogue trip through the bolongs, timed with the tide.
- **Lunch**: grilled fish or yassa.
- **Afternoon**: crossing to Mar Lodj and a walk or cart ride.
- **Evening**: sunset over the delta, then dinner with a local family.

## Sunday: slow pace and return

Sleep in, swim or walk around the village, then leave early afternoon to reach Dakar before dark [to check].

## Where to stay

A villa with a pool for a group of friends, an eco-lodge by the bolongs for a couple. Our “bolong weekend” stay combines lodging, pirogue trip and meals.
